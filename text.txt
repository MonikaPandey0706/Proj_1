May 28 has witnessed many significant historical events across different eras. Here are a few notable ones:

585 BC – A solar eclipse occurred during a battle between the Lydians and the Medes, leading to a truce.

1830 – The Indian Removal Act was signed into law by U.S. President Andrew Jackson, leading to the forced relocation of Native American tribes.

1953 – Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay became the first climbers to reach the summit of Mount Everest.

1999 – The International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia indicted Slobodan Milosevic for war crimes
